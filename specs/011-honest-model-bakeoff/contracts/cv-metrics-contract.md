# Contract: Grouped CV, Fold-Local Pipeline, and Metrics

## Split Contract

- Splits are generated by repeat and fold.
- Participant ids are the grouping unit.
- No participant id may appear in both training and validation in the same fold.
- Stratification by event label is attempted where class counts permit.
- Group isolation takes precedence over stratification.
- Fold reductions or infeasible split conditions are recorded in warnings.

## Fold-Local Pipeline Contract

- Feature matrices are split before preprocessing.
- Imputation is fit on training rows only.
- Scaling is fit on training rows only.
- Feature selection, if added, is fit on training rows only.
- Resampling, if enabled, is applied only to training rows after the split.
- Validation rows are transformed through the trained fold pipeline.
- Raw EDA and source dataframes are never mutated by modeling preprocessing.

## Threshold Contract

- Default-threshold predictions use `0.50`.
- Selected thresholds are tuned inside the training fold only.
- With `threshold_selection: inner_cv`, choose the threshold that maximizes recall subject to
  configured minimum precision.
- Ties among target-satisfying thresholds break by higher precision, then higher threshold.
- If no threshold satisfies the precision target, choose the highest-precision threshold,
  break ties by higher recall then higher threshold, and record that the precision target was
  unmet.
- Outer validation labels are never used to choose selected thresholds.

## Primary Metrics Contract

- AUPRC, recall at fixed precision, and Brier score are primary.
- Recall at fixed precision defaults to precision >= 0.80.
- Calibration intercept and slope are reported where estimable.
- Expected calibration error is reported when implemented and otherwise marked unavailable.
- AUROC may be reported only as secondary.
- Accuracy must not be reported as a headline or primary metric.

## Confidence Interval Contract

- Metric summaries use available fold/repeat metric values.
- Bootstrap CIs resample fold/repeat values per model and metric.
- `n_bootstrap` and CI level come from the effective config.
- Degenerate CI cases are marked unavailable with notes.

## Degenerate Fold Contract

- If a training fold has one class, the model/fold result is unavailable with notes.
- If a validation fold has one class and a metric cannot be estimated, that metric is
  unavailable with notes.
- If a model cannot emit probability-like scores, it is rejected or wrapped before metric
  computation.
- No metric may be silently fabricated from invalid predictions or labels.
